1.  **The Self-Attention Mechanism:** The architecture replaces traditional recurrent (RNN) or convolutional (CNN) layers with a self-attention mechanism. This allows the model to directly model dependencies between all positions in a sequence regardless of their distance, enabling better capture of long-range context compared to sequential processing.
2.  **Parallelization:** By eliminating the sequential nature of RNNs, the Transformer allows for significantly higher degrees of parallelization during training. Because the model processes the entire input sequence simultaneously rather than step-by-step, it can leverage modern hardware (like GPUs) much more efficiently, leading to faster training times on large datasets.
3.  **Positional Encoding:** Since the architecture does not inherently process data in a sequential order, it introduces positional encodings to inject information about the relative or absolute position of tokens in the sequence. This ensures the model retains the necessary structural information about the order of the input, which is critical for tasks like language translation and understanding.
